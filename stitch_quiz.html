Question 4 of 10

Which branch of the U.S. government has the power to declare war?

Understanding the separation of powers is crucial to grasping how the federal government operates in times of conflict.

lightbulb

Excellent! That's correct.

Article I, Section 8, Clause 11 of the U.S. Constitution grants Congress the power to declare war. The President is the Commander in Chief of the armed forces, but cannot officially declare war without Congressional approval.
